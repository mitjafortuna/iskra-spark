Privacy Policy – Iskra & Spark uploader

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Iskra & Spark uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Iskra & Spark - Space for Kids" (@IskraAndSpark) to upload and manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube API Services.

What data it uses

Only the owner's own YouTube channel information and videos, accessed with the owner's permission through Google OAuth. It does not access, collect or store data about any other person, including viewers of our videos.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared or sent anywhere except to Google.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or passed to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
